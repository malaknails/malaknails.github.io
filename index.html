About

Hi, I'm Malak.

Malak Nails is a small private studio where every appointment gets full, unhurried attention. From a clean solid gel colour to a full set of extensions with custom art, each set is made to suit your hands, your style and your everyday life.

Your nail health always comes first: gentle prep, quality products and careful removal, so your natural nails stay strong between sets.

Good to know

How do I book?

Use the booking form on this page or message Malak on WhatsApp at +961 71 083 936. She will reply with an available time.

What if I need to cancel or I am running late?

Please message as early as possible so your slot can go to someone else. If you are running late, let Malak know and she will do her best to fit you in.

How long will my gel or extensions last?

Most sets last around 3 to 4 weeks with good aftercare. Book a refill rather than picking them off, to keep your natural nails healthy.

Can I bring my own design idea?

Yes, please do. Send inspiration photos when you book so there is enough time set aside for your design.

Should I come with clean nails?

Please come without polish if you can. If you have gel or extensions from elsewhere, book a removal with your new set.
